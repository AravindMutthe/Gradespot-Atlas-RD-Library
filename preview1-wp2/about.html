About the Atlas

The Aravind Systems Atlas is the author's research library on systems: computing hardware, networks and infrastructure, institutions and markets, and industrial evolution — read as a story, inspected as an engineer.

Research edition · Release v1-consolidated-001 · Version 1.0.0-continuing · 57 chapters · 136 images · 22 diagrams · 48 tables · SHA-256 970489efd3899d29

Value proposition

  • One library, two ways to read. Follow a chapter as a narrative, or inspect its diagrams, tables, related chapters and original notes like an engineer checking a claim.
  • Provenance on every page. Source editions are named; originals are preserved as-is; editorial additions are labelled editorial.
  • Honest about gaps. Missing fields are shown as missing. Unverified transcription is labelled unverified. Excluded items are recorded, not silently dropped.

What this preview is — and is not

  • Preview 1 (WP2) covers the homepage, global navigation and design references only.
  • Full chapter readers, full search (WP5), the full System Explorer, learning-path progress and Labs are later work packages.
  • Labs: planned. It has no destination in this preview and is not in the navigation.
  • AI features are not in the navigation in this preview.
  • The final design theme (Alternative A, B or C) is the owner's call — this preview defaults to A for comparison and does not declare a winner.

Design references

Alternative A (navy/blue/teal, light canvas) is the preview default, with Alternative C's long-form reading qualities (generous measure, serif headings, calm paper background). Alternative B modernises the V1 design (dark header, paper gradient, V1 teal). See Compare A / B / C.
